Life OS Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Life OS is a private tool that Ed Gomberg built and runs for himself. It connects to Google accounts he owns or manages. It is not a public product, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it accesses

When an account is connected, Life OS can access that account's Gmail messages, labels and drafts, and its Google Calendar. It only touches accounts that have been signed in by their owner.

How the data is used

Only to help Ed read, search, organize and draft his own email and manage his calendar. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train general AI models.

Where the data goes

Sign-in tokens are stored in encrypted form on Ed's own computer. There is no Life OS server and no Life OS database of your email.

When Ed asks an AI assistant to work on an email, that content is sent to the AI provider he uses, currently Anthropic, to complete the request.

Data is never sold. It is not shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Google user data

Life OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Any connected account can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access can also be removed on request, and the stored token is then deleted.
